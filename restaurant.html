About Us

Kinjo's restaurant was founded in 2010 by Chef Kinjo, who wanted to share his passion for authentic Kenyan cuisine with the world. What began as a small food stall in downtown Nairobi has grown into a beloved local institution. Today, we serve over 200 customers daily from our Westlands location. Every dish uses fresh ingredients from Wakulima Market, and every recipe comes from Chef Kinjo's own kitchen.
